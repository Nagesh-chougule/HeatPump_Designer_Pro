Preliminary design tool. Refrigerant properties come from the Peng–Robinson equation of state with estimated ideal-gas heat capacities, which is typically within a few percent for pressures and 3 to 10 percent for latent heat near high reduced temperatures. Compression is adiabatic with a fixed isentropic efficiency per stage, the economizer vapour is taken as saturated, pressure drops are ignored, and costs, fuel prices and emission factors are editable assumptions. Confirm with REFPROP or CoolProp and manufacturer data before purchase.
